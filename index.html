Governance & compliance · project

ISO 27001 compliance & gap analysis

I assessed security controls against ISO/IEC 27001, identified where current practice fell short of the standard, and wrote actionable remediation plans for each gap.

  1. Scope and assets. Define the ISMS boundary and the assets in it.
  2. Assess controls. Compare current practice with each Annex A requirement.
  3. Rate every gap. Compliant, partial or missing, with a risk priority.
  4. Remediation plan. A fix, an owner and a deadline for each gap.
